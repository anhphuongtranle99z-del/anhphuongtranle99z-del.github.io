AkzAgency

A publishing tool for uploading videos to YouTube channels

What AkzAgency does

AkzAgency helps the AkzAgency content team publish videos to the YouTube channels it owns or manages. Instead of uploading each video by hand in YouTube Studio, a team member picks a folder of finished videos, and AkzAgency uploads them to the chosen channel on a schedule.

For each video, AkzAgency:

  1. Uploads the video file to the channel (as private, unlisted, public or scheduled, as chosen by the team member).
  2. Writes a title, description and tags in the channel's language, drafted from the video's own subtitle text.
  3. Sets the custom thumbnail image that our team designed for that video.

How a channel is connected

A channel is connected only when its owner signs in with their Google account and approves access on Google's consent screen. The owner can disconnect at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.

Google data AkzAgency uses

PermissionWhy AkzAgency needs it
youtube.upload
Manage your YouTube videos		Upload videos and set their thumbnails on the authorized channel.
youtube.readonly
View your YouTube account		Read the channel's name, ID and subscriber count, so the team can confirm the right channel was connected.

AkzAgency does not ask for access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any other Google service, and does not read your email address. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Responsible use of AI

AkzAgency uses an AI model (Google Gemini) only to draft text: titles, descriptions and tags, based on the subtitle text of our own videos. AkzAgency does not generate, edit or alter images or video with AI, and it is never used to create sexual, intimate or non-consensual imagery of any person. No Google user data obtained through YouTube sign-in is sent to the AI model.

Who uses AkzAgency

AkzAgency is operated by the AkzAgency team for the channels it owns or manages, together with the owners of those channels. This page and the Privacy Policy are public and can be read without signing in.
