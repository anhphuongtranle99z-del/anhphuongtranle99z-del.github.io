AkzAgency

Internal YouTube upload tool

AkzAgency is an internal tool used by our team to upload videos to YouTube channels that the team owns or manages. A channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission; the tool then uploads videos, writes titles and descriptions, and sets thumbnails on that channel only.

The tool is not offered to the public. Access is limited to accounts created by our administrator.

Privacy Policy
