Privacy Policy

AkzAgency · Last updated: 20 November 2026

AkzAgency is an internal tool that uploads videos to YouTube channels owned or managed by our team. This policy explains what Google user data the tool accesses and how it is used.

Data we access

  • YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload): to upload videos and set thumbnails on the channel you authorize.
  • YouTube read-only permission (youtube.readonly): to read the name, ID and subscriber count of the channel you authorize, so our staff can confirm it is the correct channel.
  • An OAuth refresh token issued by Google for the authorized channel.

We do not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any other Google service.

How we use the data

The data is used only to upload our team's videos to the channel you authorized and to show that channel's name inside the tool. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train AI models.

Storage and security

Refresh tokens are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and stored on our own server, which is not publicly accessible. Only our administrator and the staff member assigned to the channel can use it within the tool.

Sharing

We do not share Google user data with third parties. Video files and their text are sent to Google (YouTube, and Google Gemini to draft titles and descriptions) only as needed to perform the upload.

Retention and deletion

A channel's token is kept until the channel is removed from the tool or you revoke access. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the token then stops working immediately. To ask us to delete stored data, contact us at the address below.

Limited Use

AkzAgency's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
