AkzAgency Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information AkzAgency ("we", "us") collects when a YouTube channel owner connects their channel to AkzAgency, how we use and protect that information, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and how you can have it deleted. AkzAgency is a tool that uploads videos, writes titles and descriptions, and sets thumbnails on YouTube channels whose owners authorize it. See our home page for a description of the app.

1. Information we collect

a. Information from your Google account

When you connect a YouTube channel, you sign in with Google and approve the following permissions. We receive only the data listed here.

Google permissionData we receive
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonlyYour channel's ID, name and subscriber count.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.uploadPermission to upload videos and set thumbnails on your channel. We do not read your existing videos, comments, analytics or revenue.
OAuth tokensAn access token and refresh token issued by Google, which let AkzAgency act on your channel within the permissions above.

We do not receive your Google password, email address, contacts, Gmail, Google Drive files, payment details or location.

b. Information created while using AkzAgency

For each upload we keep a record of the video file name, the title and description written for it, the YouTube video ID, the scheduled publish time and the upload status, so our team can see what has been published.

c. Team member accounts

Our own team members sign in to AkzAgency with a username and password created by our administrator. Passwords are stored only as Argon2 hashes.

2. How we use the information

  • Channel ID, name and subscriber count: shown inside AkzAgency so our team can confirm the correct channel was connected and choose it when uploading.
  • Upload permission and OAuth tokens: used only to upload the videos our team selects to your channel, set their titles, descriptions, tags and privacy status, and set their thumbnails.
  • Upload records: used to show upload history and avoid uploading the same video twice.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not use it to build user profiles, and we do not use it to develop, improve or train AI or machine learning models.

3. AI processing

AkzAgency uses Google Gemini to draft video titles, descriptions and tags. The only data sent to Gemini is the subtitle text of our own videos and the public title, description and tags of a public YouTube video we choose as a reference. No Google user data obtained through your sign-in (channel information or tokens) is ever sent to Gemini or any other AI model. AkzAgency does not generate or edit images or video with AI.

4. How we store and protect the information

  • OAuth tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before being stored in our database. The encryption key is kept separately from the database.
  • The database is stored on a server we control. It is not publicly accessible.
  • Only our administrator and the team member assigned to a channel can use that channel inside AkzAgency, and every access requires signing in.
  • Data is sent only to Google services (YouTube Data API, Google OAuth and Google Gemini) over encrypted HTTPS connections.

5. Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent or transfer Google user data to any third party. Data is sent to Google only as needed to perform the actions described above. We may disclose information only if required by law.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Your channel information and OAuth tokens are kept only while your channel is connected to AkzAgency.
  • If you revoke access, the stored token stops working immediately and can no longer be used on your channel.
  • When you ask us to disconnect your channel, we delete the stored token and channel information from our database.
  • Daily database backups are kept for 7 days and then deleted automatically, so deleted data disappears from backups within 7 days.
  • Upload records for videos that remain published on your channel are kept for our team's publishing history unless you ask us to delete them.

How to revoke access: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions, select AkzAgency and choose "Remove access". AkzAgency then immediately loses access to your channel.

How to request deletion: contact us using the details below. We will delete your data within 30 days of the request and confirm when it is done.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

AkzAgency's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. AkzAgency also uses YouTube API Services; by connecting a channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Children

AkzAgency is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

For questions or deletion requests, open an issue at github.com/anhphuongtranle99z-del/anhphuongtranle99z-del.github.io/issues.
